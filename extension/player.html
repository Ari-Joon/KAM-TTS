Model Tuning

Live · applies to the next chunk
Temperature —
How adventurous the voice is. Low (~0.2) = precise, steady, can sound flat. High (~0.7) = expressive and varied, but risks slips or drift. Sweet spot for reading: 0.30–0.45.
Top-K —
How many candidate sounds the model may choose from at each step. Lower = safer, more repetitive. Higher = more natural variety. Typical: 30–60.
Top-P —
Keeps only the most likely options that together cover this probability mass. 0.9 trims unlikely, odd sounds while keeping natural range. Lower = tighter/safer, higher = looser.
Repetition penalty —
Discourages the voice from looping or stuttering on the same sound. Higher = fewer repeats but can clip pacing; too low = stutters. Typical: 3–5.
Volume —
Playback loudness of the spoken audio. Local playback only — doesn't change the model. 0% mutes, 100% is unaltered, above 100% boosts (up to 600%) with soft-clip + limiter so louder never distorts.
Frees GPU memory after this long with no speech, then wakes automatically on your next play (a brief warm-up the first time). Always on keeps the model loaded for instant response. Saves memory during long pauses — best set to how long you typically step away.

📖 What these stats mean
Total ChunksDistinct sentences synthesised and stored. One reading adds its sentences once (re-reads don't double-count).
Avg QualityMean quality score across analysed chunks. Combines Whisper word-accuracy, pitch variation, energy and voice consistency. Higher is better.
High / Low QualityChunks scoring ≥85% (High) or <60% (Low). A few Lows are normal — usually short headings or numbers Whisper struggles to transcribe, not actual bad audio.
Success / ErrorsChunks that synthesised cleanly vs. ones that threw an error. Errors should be rare.
SkippedChunks suppressed on purpose (a SUPPRESS rule) — emitted as silence rather than spoken.
Analysed by WhisperHow many chunks the local Whisper model has listened back to. Analysis runs in the background after each chunk.
FlaggedChunks where analysis found something worth noting (skip, hallucination, cut-off, drift). These populate the Whisper Analysis view.
Reports / Active rules / BlacklistedReports you've filed, rules currently shaping audio, and tokens stripped before synthesis.
Whisper · librosaThe two local analysis engines: Whisper checks what words were heard; librosa measures how it sounded (pitch, energy, rhythm).
Pitch meanAverage fundamental frequency of your cloned voice, in Hz. Part of the baseline used to detect voice drift.
Energy meanAverage loudness/intensity of the baseline voice. Sharp drops mid-chunk signal a cut-off.
Speaking rateSyllable onsets per second — how fast the baseline voice speaks. Used to spot chunks that rushed or dragged.
Baseline samplesHow many confirmed-good chunks the voice baseline is built from. It firms up after ~10 high-quality chunks, making drift detection more reliable.

📖 Report Guide
How reporting works: pick the issue type, fill the fields the way the examples below show, and submit. High-confidence reports apply to your audio immediately and become permanent user-confirmed rules (see the AI tab). You usually don't need to report — KAM auto-fixes most issues. Report when something is persistently wrong.
⚠ The model reads AI, API, SDK, GPU naturally. Only report if it genuinely sounded wrong.
🗣 PRONUNCIATION WRONG
A specific word or abbreviation was mispronounced. The fix is written to the pronunciation store and applied on every future chunk.
Issue type:🗣 Pronunciation wrong
Problem token:SQL
Should sound like:sequel
Actually sounded like:ess cue ell
Action:📖 Add to pronunciation store
Confidence:🟢 High — applies immediately
Token = exact word as it appeared in text. Should sound like = the natural spoken words you want.

💭 HALLUCINATION — words added that were not in text
The model spoke words that were never in the source text. This flags the pattern and tightens temperature.

Confidence:🟡 Medium
Fill in the repeated or added word. The model flags this pattern and reduces temperature.

Console tags

[PROSODY] sentence type and pause length chosen
[SKIP] chunk skipped — too short or code-only
[CODE] code or symbol token converted to speech

[LEARNER] quality analysis or learning event
[RETRY] retrying after a recoverable error
[ERROR] synthesis failed — will retry
[FATAL] unrecoverable, e.g. a CUDA crash
[WARN] non-fatal warning, e.g. text truncated
[BOOT] server importing libraries at launch
[STARTUP] model loading, latents and warmup
[STANDBY] model evicted to save VRAM, or waking
[MATH] chunk contains an equation or specialised characters
[HOST] power-button launcher messages
Rule types
PRONUNCIATION say a word differently
PUNCT correct punctuation or phrasing
SPLIT force a chunk break at this point
BOUNDARY adjust where chunks start and end
BLACKLIST strip this from the text entirely
SUPPRESS never speak this chunk
FLAG watch only — noticed, no change made
MATH a taught equation reading, applied before the symbol expander
Quality flags
WHISPER_SKIP — Whisper heard fewer words than the text had
LOWCONF — transcription confidence was low
RUSHED — spoken faster than your baseline pace
VOICE_DRIFT — voice characteristics moved away from your clone
CUTOFF — audio ended before the text did
Content facets
A chunk is often several things at once — a definition containing an equation, a list item with a citation. Facets are detected independently and shown on each chunk in the Live Feed.
∑ math equations or specialised symbols
• list bulleted or numbered items
≡ definition a term being defined
⌘ code code or API references
❝ quote quoted or reported speech
¶ citation academic references
A trailing +2 means two further facets were detected. Only math gets its own learned settings — the others are recorded for analysis, because every extra learning bucket thins the evidence in it.
Chunk fingerprint terms
simple / typical / dense — overall complexity band
short / medium / long — chunk length in words
clean / comma-heavy / complex — punctuation shape, which drives pausing
plain / technical / symbolic — wording difficulty, which drives pronunciation care
KAM learns voice settings per fingerprint, so a fix for dense comma-heavy sentences doesn't disturb short plain ones.
